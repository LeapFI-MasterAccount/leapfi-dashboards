Value-model formulas

sealed math
Build = hours × rate × local
Annual value = affected hrs × role rate × local × realization
Payback (mo) = build ÷ annual × 12
3-yr ROI = 3 × annual ÷ build
Qualifies when 3-yr ROI ≥ the qualifying floor

Only the inputs are operator-adjustable; the formulas are fixed. Per-opportunity numbers are produced by the run, not entered here.
